Modifications by English Assist contributors (2026):
- Extract separate English POS, Chinese definitions and IPA fields from ECDICT.
- Preserve CC-CEDICT Chinese headwords, original numbered-tone readings and
  source glosses; derive lowercase untoned keys for lookup without discarding
  the headword/sense evidence in each mapping.
- Link eligible English heads from CC-CEDICT glosses to English entries; also
  reverse exact complete Chinese gloss items in ECDICT through the known
  CC-CEDICT headwords/readings. This is not arbitrary Chinese substring matching.
- Attach Wanxiang Chinese headword/reading frequencies and source/usage flags
  and sense ordinals. Rank word-level relations before English-frequency tie
  breaks, distinguish exact pinyin from longer-word expansion, and bound lookup.
- Compile the independent indexes and evidence into english-lexicon-v2.bin
  (RTENLX02): word annotations plus Chinese headword/reading/gloss/frequency/source
  relations. The English frequency image remains a separate component.

Reproducibility:
The source distribution includes losslessly compressed originals and a standalone
Python compiler in tools/english-assist/build-lexicon.py. Frozen repository commits,
original Git blob IDs, compressed/content SHA256 values and license SHA256 values
are recorded in data/dict/raw/english-assist/SOURCES.json (installed as
english-lexicon-sources.json). The actual output digest/counts are recorded in
english-lexicon-v2.json beside the binary. Preserve the source manifest, all data
notices and complete licenses when redistributing this metadata.

Separate limited modern-entity seed
The 51 reviewed company/product/developer-tool entries are maintained
separately in data/dict/modern-entities.json and the embedded EAMODEN1 TSV. Their
structured source is Wikidata (CC0-1.0, https://www.wikidata.org/wiki/Wikidata:Licensing).
Each approved entry records a Wikidata QID, exact revision, revision URL and an
official-site URL; aliases and Chinese summaries are reviewed for this small seed.
For example, Anthropic is linked to Q116758847 at revision 2562358338
(https://www.wikidata.org/w/index.php?title=Q116758847&oldid=2562358338) and its
official company page https://www.anthropic.com/company.
Official sites are identity references, not a claim that their prose, logos or
other assets are CC0 or copied into this product. Frozen structured source and
provenance are in data/dict/raw/modern-entities/; build.json records the actual
count and approved JSON/runtime TSV digests. This is not a complete Wikidata or
current-entity dictionary and does not update automatically while typing.
The independent catalog tools/runtime code remain MIT; this separate CC0 seed
does not replace or change the lexicon licenses above.
