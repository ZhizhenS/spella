English Assist reviewed modern-name seed, schema EAMODEN1

This is a limited editorial seed of 51 AI companies/products and developer-tool
brands, not a complete Wikidata dump or a comprehensive contemporary dictionary.
Entity suggestions remain separate from ordinary English adjective/noun senses.
No catalog lookup performs network requests or opens a file while typing.

Structured source: Wikidata, https://www.wikidata.org/
Data license: CC0 1.0 Universal, https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/
Wikidata policy: https://www.wikidata.org/wiki/Wikidata:Licensing
Data access: https://www.wikidata.org/wiki/Wikidata:Data_access

wikidata-snapshot.json.gz is a small projection of real MediaWiki wbgetentities
responses: IDs, last revision numbers, modification times, labels, descriptions,
instance-of IDs and official-website claims. It records the original upstream
response digest and the extraction date. It is not an original full response.
Each approved entry also links its exact revision and an official company or
product page. No Q identifiers were invented from names or inferred from a
third-party search snippet.

Short Chinese descriptions and normalized input aliases are reviewed editorial
adaptations of the frozen structured facts, with supplementary official-site
identity checks. These catalog contributions are also provided under CC0 1.0.
Website links are factual provenance; website HTML, logos and marketing text are
not incorporated. Names may be trademarks of their respective owners. No source
project, company or product endorses English Assist.

In particular, Codex refers to the software engineering agent Q138940795, not
the older code-generating model Q108582200. OpenAI's entry avoids repeating the
outdated nonprofit-only Chinese description in the Wikidata snapshot; its short
company description is checked against https://openai.com/about/.

Approved catalog: data/dict/modern-entities.json
Embedded runtime projection: data/dict/modern-entities.tsv
Offline consistency check: tools/english-assist/modern-entities.py --check
Explicit upstream review proposal (does not change approved files):
  tools/english-assist/modern-entities.py --propose-refresh <separate-review.json>

Normal builds and typing never refresh upstream data automatically. A future
update requires review of identity, revisions, aliases, official pages and
Chinese descriptions, followed by explicit --write-runtime and --check.
