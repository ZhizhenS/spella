## Feature

- Confirm separate English words with spaces added only before them when needed, leaving the cursor directly after the last word.
- Confirm joined English such as `iamrobot` as `I am robot` without predicting untyped letters.
- Check capitalization and input options on a draggable floating status bar, and tap Shift to toggle automatic spacing.

## Improvement

- Press Enter during composition to commit exactly what you typed without accepting a suggestion or submitting the application.
