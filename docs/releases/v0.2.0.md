## Feature

- See Chinese definitions, parts of speech and optional phonetics with offline English suggestions, or find English words by typing pinyin.
- Add, edit, delete, import and export personal words, with a previous-version backup and no automatic collection of typed words.
- Customize candidate themes, font size, width, spacing and visible dictionary fields.

## Improvement

- Read five candidates per page, use `1`–`5` to select a visible word, and use `-` / `_` or `=` / `+` to turn pages.
- Find input, appearance, shortcuts, personal words and AI settings in grouped Chinese pages, with selected-text AI edits retaining preview and explicit confirmation.
- Accept words with Tab or a click without an added space, while digits and page symbols keep their normal behavior when no candidates are shown.

## Fix

- Confirm an explicitly selected candidate with Enter without also submitting a message, search or newline.
- Register the English keyboard correctly when installing from desktop applications.
- Load bundled word meanings correctly in Notepad and stop background helpers during uninstall.
