## Feature

- Find the word you meant from a spelling that only sounds right, such as "fone", "nite" or "definately".
- Type contractions without the apostrophe: "dont" offers "don't" and "im" offers "I'm".

## Improvement

- See the likely correction first when what you typed is not a word, instead of after every other candidate.
- Keep everyday words on the first page, ahead of rare names, slang and archaic words.
- Show names, weekdays and brands with their usual capital letters.
- Remove older installed versions automatically after an upgrade.

## Fix

- Stop pinyin lookups from replacing English words you are still typing, such as "fo" for "for".
