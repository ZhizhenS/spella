## Feature

- Find everyday things whose English is two words from their pinyin, such as "shouji" for "cell phone" and "xinyongka" for "credit card".

## Improvement

- Put the common Chinese word first when several share the same pinyin, such as "wanshang" for "evening" and "hushi" for "nurse".
- Show the pinyin meaning first when the letters are also a rare English word, such as "canting" for "restaurant".

## Fix

- Keep spelling suggestions from pushing pinyin meanings down the list.
